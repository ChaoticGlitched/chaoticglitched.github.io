Grindspots are spots where high-level mobs spawn that give a lot of XP. Listed below are the best ones for each level range.

For levels 1 to 10, I recommend either just doing quests - as they're very short this early and grant a sizable amount of XP - or grinding at the Zombee Tree grindspot at Nivla Woods, at -355, 64, -1413.

For levels 10 to 20, I recommend either just doing quests (again) or grinding at the Black Road, at 647, 61, -1530.

For levels 20 to 30, I recommend grinding either at Mount Wynn, at -54, 66, -1977, or Saint's Row in Nemract, at 309, 46, -2029.

For levels 30 to 60, I recommend grinding at the Llevigar Spider Nest, at -2147, 56, -4721. Party finder parties will often be grinding here, so keep a lookout for ones you can join.

For levels 60 to 80, I recommend grinding at the Flesh Cave, at -997, 46, -5559.
While party finder parties commonly exist, I actually recommend doing this solo, because the mobs don't give that much XP and you don't want your party members to take the limited XP. Additionally, the mobs don't do that much damage and there are several sections where you can avoid all damage altogether.

For levels 80 to 105, where else but the Scrapyard, in Corkus at -1445, 72, -2522. The Scrapyard is immensely dangerous (even without a mob totem), and as such I highly recommend joining a party finder party.

For levels 105 to 120, I recommend the Bat Cave in the recently released Fruma, at -1689, 4, -936. Party finder parties are almost always available for Bat Cave, so be sure to join one!
