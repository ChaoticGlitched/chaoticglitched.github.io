General Speedleveling

What Is Speedleveling?

Wynncraft is a massive MMORPG, all inside of Minecraft. It has hours and hours of content... but sometimes, once you've already seen that content, you don't want to see it again.
Speedleveling is the process of grinding out mobs in order to level up a class to the max rapidly. It is also called power leveling. Knowing how to properly speedlevel can be difficult! So, this guide has been assembled in order to help you understand what to do.
In this guide, we'll go over proper equipment, where to go, and what archetypes to choose. This page will go over the general information you need to speedlevel; links to the more specific things I just listed can be found at the top of this page.

This is a callout. They're used to explain things that aren't directly related to the main information of the page. Pay attention to them!

How to Speedlevel

The essence of speedleveling is very simple. It goes as such:

  • Go to a grindspot.
  • Kill the mobs that spawn there for the experience points, or xp.
  • Once you level up past the viable range the grindspot can be used in, find a new grindspot.
  • Repeat until you hit the level cap.

It's as simple as that!

Mob Totems

Speedleveling isn't easy. It's time-consuming, boring, and can be - and more importantly, feel - slow. Grindspots can easily kill you if you aren't prepared, and proper equipment is nearly mandatory to speed up the process.
Most importantly, however, are Mob Totems.

Mob totems massively increase the rate of mob spawns. As such, they increase the rate of xp gained massively. Mob totems last for five minutes and can generally encompass an entire grindspot. Additionally, if someone else drops the totem, you benefit from it too as long as you're in range!

Mob totems are acquired from ranks, which can be purchased from the Wynncraft shop. They are replenished every day, and the higher your rank, the more you get each day. It should be noted that mob totems cannot be acquired any other way.

But be warned! It is very easy for mobs to swarm you if you're not careful, and then you'll die. To mitigate this, it is highly recommended that you join a party.

Parties

Parties are groups of up to 10 players. Alongside other things, such as xp being shared, their primary purpose is to gather multiple groups of players in the same world, in the same spot, to do the same thing.
The immediate benefit of this is that the extra mobs from mob totems won't be as dangerous, as they will be killed almost immediately from the huge amount of players that are attacking them.
Additionally, parties often contain players who are healers. This makes it so that you do not need to bring healing potions or other methods of healing, as you will be healed by the players nearby.
It is very easy to find parties on the Party Finder.

The party finder catalogues all public parties, many of which tend to be speedleveling parties. The party finder can be opened by using the command /partyfinder or /pfinder. Joining a party requires three things:

  1. You are of the required level,
  2. The party is not full,
  3. And the world is not full.

Fortunately, you can always get around the world being full by using /lobby and switching to that world, or if you have Hero Rank or higher, using /switch to switch directly. Neat!

If all these conditions are met, you will join the public party.

Bombs

Occasionally, people will throw bombs, which commonly last for 20 minutes. These bombs have effects such as increasing loot gain, making gathering profession materials faster, or - most important for our purposes - doubling all xp gain.
Unfortunately, these bombs must be bought with real money. Thankfully, everybody on the world can benefit from bombs thrown!

Parties on the party finder are often on double xp worlds, so joining a party on the party finder can help you find these worlds faster.

Players with Champion Rank have access to a bombbell, which allows them to see all the worlds that have bombs on them, as well as which bombs and how long they will last for. Large guilds commonly have many players with champion rank online, and you can ask them if there are any worlds with double xp active.

Double xp is commonly abbreviated to dxp, and double combat xp is commonly abbreviated to just cxp - although people will generally assume you mean double combat xp whenever you say dxp. Double profession xp and double profession speed are commonly just called "profs. All the bombs follow this format - double loot, for example, is referred to as dloot.

And that's everything you need to know! The rest of this guide will go into far more in-depth knowledge, but from here, you can grind effectively.
Happy grinding!
