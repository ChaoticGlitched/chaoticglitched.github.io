The gear you choose to use for speedleveling is incredibly important. Not only do you need enough health to survive the difficult enemies you'll be going up against, you also need the XP gain bonus certain gear provides. I'll go over what you should wear for each level here.

Remember to save the items! You can use them again for future leveling sprees!

For levels 1-29, it really doesn't matter - pick up basically anything that works. I do highly recommend getting 2 Rarity rings, however, as they can be worn at level 1 and provide a hefty XP bonus. You can use these for the entire class!

Level 29 is about where I suggest actually putting thought into what you use. Specifically, I recommend using the Adventurer's Set, which provides a nifty +50% XP gain bonus when all four pieces are worn together. You can get the entire set off of the Trade Market for cheap.

Once you hit level 60, you have a choice. You can keep using the Adventurer's Set - which works, although not well - or you can specialize a bit.
I recommend buying the items Bad Wolf, Greaves of Honor, Geis, and Venison, which provide an absolutely nutty +134% XP gain in total at maximum (although in practice it's more like +120% with a good set).
You can buy all of these items off the Trade Market. Mind you, it's very expensive, but also very worth it!

Level 80 is where things get a bit complicated. The best thing to use here is the Cosmic Set, which can carry you from level 80 all the way to 120. A good set will provide about +150% XP gain, as well as some other buffs.
So what's the catch? Well, to get your hands on it, you need to complete the ??? quest - and you need to do it on every class you're leveling. It will take about an hour and requires four players, and can be rather convoluted and annoying. However, it is very much worth it.

I recommend using the wiki page, which will tell you what all of you need to do.
