Archetypes are Wynncraft's name for subclasses. Each one radically shifts the way the class is played - Assassin's Acrobat, for example, plays wildly different from Trickster, which plays wildly different from Shadestepper.
For speedleveling, generally, the most important thing is good crowd control. All five are gone into in more depth below.

The Archer is very much a glass cannon. The archer has very low health, but very high DPS, as well as inherently ranged attacks. As such, the absolute best way to play the archer is to remain at a distance from all enemies.
The three archetypes for Archer are Boltslinger, Sharpshooter, and Trapper. Boltslinger and Sharpshooter are opposites; Boltcaster is a very "spray and pray" type of archetype, while Sharpshooter is all about lining up your shots.
Trapper, meanwhile, is all about crowd control, and as such is the best option for speedleveling. Trapper involves setting up traps that are triggered when a mob touches them. These traps get stronger over time. Additionally, Trapper can summon animals that will damage enemies for you. Both of these are excellent crowd control, which makes Trapper the objective best Archer archetype for speedleveling.

The Warrior is Wynncraft's tank. The Warrior's archetypes are the Fallen, Battle Monk, and Paladin, which is tricky because none of them have very good crowd control.
The Paladin is THE tank, while Battle Monk is more mobility-focused and the Fallen trades health for damage. Not very helpful.
However, due to the high damage and defense of the Warrior, it is possible to kill large amounts of enemies nearly at once by simply going fast enough. As such, the mobility-focused Battle Monk is what you should go into to speedlevel.

The Assassin is, to put it simply, a rogue. They are very much a shock attacker, with - similar to the Warrior - very little crowd control, instead almost entirely focused on just one target.
The first Archetype is Shadestepper, which allows for taking your time and then delivering massive burst damage to one enemy. Given that we are trying to kill as many enemies as fast as possible, this is not optimal.
That leaves Trickster and Acrobat. Acrobat, while it does move around the battlefield and rely heavily on the Spin Attack spell - the closest thing to AoE the Assassin has - it simply does not do enough damage until you can unlock the Jasmine Bloom ability.
Trickster, meanwhile is basically a flat damage and health increase with some gimmicks. As such, the best bet is to go into Trickster until you get enough ability points to unlock Jasmine Bloom, and then switch to Acrobat.

The Mage is characterized by the spells they cast. In a turn of good news for us, the damage spells are AoE based - Meteor in a circle and Ice snake in a line - and their utility spell, Heal, heals not just the mage but also party members.
This is golden for speedleveling. The AoE of the damage spells makes for good crowd control, while healing keeps the entire party alive. Having your party alive means you can withstand a Mob Totem, meaning you can kill more mobs.
So, archetypes. Arcanist can't heal, and it's a bit difficult to use, so I'm going to discard it. That leaves Lightbender and Riftwalker. Lightbender is entirely support-based, which means that while you're keeping your team alive, you can't kill mobs very effectively.
That leaves Riftwalker, which - fortunately - is very excellent for speedleveling. Riftwalker has Heal, deals a ton of damage, and is just generally good. As such, I recommend Riftwalker for Mage.

The Shaman is basically built for speedleveling. The Shaman's abilities are entirely built around its totem, a throwable object that emits AoE damage and healing. Absolutely brilliant.
The three archetypes are really just two - Ritualist isn't very good. That leaves Acolyte and Summoner. Acolyte is good, but it's very difficult to use. Summoner, meanwhile, lets you just throw down totems and let your summons do the job of getting XP for you. And, to sweeten the deal, your totems also heal your party! Trust me, use Summoner. You won't regret it.
